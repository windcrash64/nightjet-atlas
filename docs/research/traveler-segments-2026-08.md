# Real traveler segments for European rail / night-train planning
Research date: 2026-08-31. Primary sources only; Reddit was inaccessible to tooling (see Gaps).

## Verified numbers
- 1,237,000 Interrail+Eurail passes sold 2023, +25% YoY, record. (interrail.com press room, spring-promotion-2024)
- Split: 797,000+ Interrail / 439,000+ Eurail; 140,000+ DiscoverEU. (same)
- DiscoverEU: ~36,000 passes Apr 2025 round; 40,000 in 2026 round; next round 1-15 Oct 2026. (youth.europa.eu/discovereu_en)
- Youth pass = age 12-27, 25% off. NO student/enrolment discount exists. (interrail.com/en/support/interested-in-interrailing/do-you-offer-any-discounts)
- Reservation fees identical for all pass ages. (interrail.com international-train-reservation-fees)
- EU rail 2024: 8.7bn trips, 444.5bn pkm, +5.8%. (Eurostat ddn-20260514-1)
- EU air 2024: 1.1bn passengers, +8.3%, above 2019. (Eurostat ddn-20251208-1)  <-- air outgrew rail
- T&E/YouGov Sep 2025, n=10,514, 7 countries: 61% of long-distance rail travellers have abandoned a journey over booking hassle; 69% among 18-24. 43% would travel more by rail if booking were easier.
- T&E Apr 2026: 20% of 30 busiest intl routes unbookable end-to-end from major operators; >half of >900km journeys unbookable in one go.
- Rail Planner app: 2.7 stars / 1.5K ratings (Apple App Store, US).
- Interrail killed its web Trip Planner 4 May 2026 (announced 28 Apr). Thread: 24 replies, 1,795 views.
- 205 regular sleeper lines Europe 2026; 5 new, 10 cut. (Back-on-Track, 2026-06-08)
- Flight Free UK: ~6,000 pledges (2019 campaign). Tiny.

## Segment verdicts
REAL, functionally distinct:
1. Pass holders (reservation-constrained routing) - biggest, best evidenced.
2. Night-train travellers (berth class = product, not filter).
DEMOTE to a preference toggle, not a mode:
3. Youth/under-27 - price-only, zero routing difference. "Student mode" is a misnomer.
4. Climate/flight-avoider - identity not behaviour; air grew faster than rail.
5. Slow-travel/sleeper-as-hotel - not a separate segment, it's the night-train value prop.
6. "Flight only" - actively wrong for this product (no fare data; see DATA_SOURCES.md).
